What I would fix first

These are hypotheses from how chit funds usually work. I would confirm them with SJV’s subscribers and agents in the first week.

Trust and transparency

Show the full passbook, the commission and the auction result for every chit, so nobody has to ask what happened.

Missed due dates

Clear reminders and one-tap UPI payment, so a late fee never comes as a surprise.

Confusing auction terms

Explain discount, dividend and prize amount in plain words, in English and regional languages.

How I would run this project

Week 1: Listen

Talk to subscribers, agents and branch staff. Map how a chit works today, from joining to prize payout.

Weeks 2–3: Design

User flows, wireframes and a clickable Figma prototype for the subscriber app and the website.

Week 4: Test and hand off

Usability tests with real subscribers, then a design system and developer-ready files.
